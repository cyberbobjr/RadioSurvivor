# Radio Survivor

Radio Survivor (`batman_RadioSurvivor`) is a Project Zomboid **Build 42.21** mod, singleplayer and multiplayer.
Knox County, July 1993: voices answer on the radio, and groups of human survivors live, fight and fortify in the
county.

> **Test version (0.3.0).** Expect bugs and balance changes. Start a new game and keep a backup of any save you care
> about.

## What the mod adds

- **Voices on the radio**: Walt, a nurse, a deserter, a besieged family, a pirate station and a smuggler, each on a
  real frequency drawn for each game. Their frequencies are found on notes in the loot. You talk to them with any
  transmitting radio held in hand or worn on the back (**Transmit** section of the radio window). They are played by
  an AI and remember you.
- **Field notebook** (**Notebook** button): contacts, radio history by frequency, missions, rewards, reputation.
- **Living Knox survivor groups**: 3 to 5 groups of human NPCs with a home, a leader, real supplies, needs and
  routines. They defend and fortify their home, fight zombies and keep living off screen. Settlers, neutrals and
  raiders.
- **Missions**: deliver, scout, find a missing person, clear a place, recover a stash, signal. The server checks every
  objective.
- **Rescue** of a besieged family, **the Ohio smuggler** (trades by radio), **evening chronicle** of the server,
  **last pages** of the dead, **reputation**.

The server decides everything: the AI only writes words and picks from closed lists.

## The bridge

The AI is reached through **the bridge**, a small Python program you start yourself on the machine that runs the
server (your PC in singleplayer). Without it the game stays playable, and the voices and NPCs stay silent.
